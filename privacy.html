Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartResume India. We respect your privacy and aim to protect the personal information you use while creating your resume.

2. Resume Information

The resume details you enter are processed in your browser to display your resume preview. The website code does not send these details to our own server or store them in our own database.

3. Profile Photos

Uploaded profile photos are read by your browser for resume preview. They are not uploaded to our own server by the website code.

4. PDF Download

You can use the print feature of your browser to print or save your resume as a PDF. Please keep your downloaded files secure.

5. Cookies and Advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if third-party services such as advertising or analytics are added in the future. We will update this policy when such services are introduced.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Third-party service providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not needed for your resume. Keep your device and downloaded resume files secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features or services change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
