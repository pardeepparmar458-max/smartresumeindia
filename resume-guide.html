How to Make a Professional Resume

A resume is a short summary of your education, skills, work experience and achievements. A clear, well-organised resume helps an employer understand your background quickly.

Use the SmartResume India resume maker to enter your details, preview your resume and save or print it as a PDF.

1. Choose a simple format

Use readable fonts, consistent headings and enough white space. Put your most recent education or work experience first. Keep the layout easy to scan.

2. Include the important sections

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, professional email and city.
  • Career objective or summary: Two or three lines about your strengths and the role you want.
  • Education: Qualification, institution, board or university, and year.
  • Work experience: Job title, employer, dates and key responsibilities. Freshers can include internships or projects.
  • Skills: Relevant skills you can confidently demonstrate.
  • Projects and certificates: Add genuine and relevant achievements.

3. Use clear, specific descriptions

Start bullet points with action words such as managed, prepared, assisted, organised or created. Include real results where possible. Never invent experience or qualifications.

4. Tailor your resume to each job

Read the job description and highlight the experience and skills that genuinely match. Use relevant terms naturally without repeating keywords unnecessarily.

5. Proofread carefully

Check spelling, phone number, email address, dates, employer names and qualification details before sending your resume.

6. Save your resume as a PDF

PDF format usually preserves the layout across devices. Use a clear filename such as YourName-Resume.pdf, and follow the employer's requested file format if it differs.

Tips for freshers

  • Highlight education, practical training, projects and relevant skills.
  • Include volunteering or coursework when it relates to the job.
  • Keep the information honest and concise.
  • Use a professional email address and a reachable phone number.
